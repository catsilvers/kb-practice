# Changing a flight on the day of travel

## Summary
Travelers can request a same-day flight change for a $75 fee when seats are available.

## When to use this article
Use this when a traveler asks to move to an earlier or later flight on their travel date.

## Steps
1. Confirm the traveler's identity and booking number.
2. Check seat availability on the requested flight.
3. Explain the $75 fee before making the change.
4. Process the change and send the updated itinerary.
